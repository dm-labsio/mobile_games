Voxel Pop

Peel a voxel object layer by layer until nothing is left.

  • Front row only. Tap a gun in the top row of the rack to holster it. The gun behind it moves up.
  • Guns only hit what faces them. Cubes on the back wait for the turntable, or spin the object yourself. A gun whose colour isn't showing waits in its holster. Five waiting guns and you're stuck.
  • Chained pairs go together: both must be in the front row, and you need two free holsters.
No losing. Stuck guns go back to the rack.

Sound starts when you tap a level. Drag the object to spin and tilt it.
